WHAT EACH ROLE CAN SEE
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Private Seller (01-05)
    - Guided 6-step listing flow
    - Seller dashboard, My Ads, lead analytics
    - Free tier limits: 1 ad per month, 3 photos
    - Can purchase a package upgrade through the UPI checkout

  Verified Dealer (06-10)
    - Everything a private seller can do
    - Pre-provisioned on the Comprehensive tier (10 ads, 8 photos)
    - Dealer storefront / micro-site at /store/:dealerId
    - Verification badge shown on listings

  Buyer/Tester (11-19)
    - Search, filter and browse the full catalogue
    - Save ads to the Saved Ads screen
    - EMI calculator, on-road price breakdown, instant valuation
    - Contact sellers, share listings

  Super Admin (20)
    - Everything above
    - Owner Super-Admin panel: dealer approvals, catalogue model
      injection, category duplication, listing moderation queue

HOW TO SIGN IN
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Open the app (or https://motora-five.vercel.app).
  2. Tap "Sell" or "Profile" in the bottom navigation.
     Browsing and search work WITHOUT signing in - the gate only appears
     on seller and account screens.
  3. Enter the email address exactly as listed above.
  4. Enter the 4-digit PIN.
  5. Tap "Sign in".

IMPORTANT NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * Only these 20 accounts can authenticate. Any other email address is
    rejected, and a wrong PIN is rejected with the same message so the
    tester list cannot be enumerated.

  * PIN changes are intentionally DISABLED on this build. Tapping
    "Forgot or change PIN?" returns an explanatory message.

  * These are demonstration credentials for closed testing only. They
    are compiled into the client bundle and must be replaced by a real
    backend before any production or open-track release. Set the
    environment variable VITE_DISABLE_TEST_ACCOUNTS=true to turn the
    hardcoded path off.

  * Google Play requires 12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days
    before you can apply for production access. Invite all 20 addresses
    in Play Console > Testing > Closed testing > Testers.
